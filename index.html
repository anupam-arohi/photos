I have been photographing the sky over southern Finland since 2011, mostly at night, mostly in the cold, and usually with nothing to show for it. What follows is the small fraction that survived.

Grouped into series rather than subjects, because what a photograph is tends to be the least interesting thing about it. Filter by subject below if you would rather browse that way.
